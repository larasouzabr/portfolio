I am a Brazilian software developer with a strong foundation in TypeScript, Java, and frontend frameworks including Vue, Angular, and React. I completed my studies in Software Engineering at the Federal University of Ceará (UFC). During my time there, I worked as a programmer and researcher on various international projects and held prestigious positions, including an Institutional Program of Scientific Initiation Scholarships (PIBIC) scholarship at UFC - Quixadá, and a developer scholar at FUNCAP. Currently, I am a direct PhD candidate in Computer Science and Computational Mathematics at the University of São Paulo (USP), at the Institute of Mathematics and Computer Sciences (ICMC). My research focuses on Code Quality and Inclusive Software Engineering — spanning code/test smell refactoring, the application of LLMs in software development, human-computer interaction, and web accessibility — with work recognized at venues including ICSE, SBES, and LLM4Code.
